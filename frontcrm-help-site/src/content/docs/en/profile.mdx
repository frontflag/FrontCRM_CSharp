## Overview

The Profile section lets you manage your account information, password, and preferences.

## Common operations

### Update personal information

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Profile**.
3. Update your name, phone, or email.
4. Click **Save**.

### Change password

1. Open **Profile > Security**.
2. Enter your current password and new password.
3. Click **Update Password**.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I change the interface language?**
A: Use the language switcher in the top-right corner of the application.
