## Overview

The Finance module handles invoices, payments, expenses, and financial reports.

## Common operations

### Create an invoice

1. Navigate to **Finance > Invoice List**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Select the customer or vendor and add invoice lines.
4. Save and issue the invoice.

### Record a payment

1. Open the invoice.
2. Click **Record Payment**.
3. Enter the payment amount, date, and method.
4. Confirm.

## FAQ

**Q: Can invoices be generated automatically from sales orders?**
A: Yes, use the **Generate Invoice** action on a confirmed sales order.
