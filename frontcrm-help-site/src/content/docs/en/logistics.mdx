## Overview

The Logistics module manages shipments, tracking numbers, and delivery status.

## Common operations

### Create a shipment

1. Navigate to **Logistics > Shipment List**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Select the sales order or purchase order to ship.
4. Enter shipping details and save.

### Track shipments

1. Open the shipment record.
2. Enter the tracking number provided by the carrier.
3. Update status as the shipment moves.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I split one sales order into multiple shipments?**
A: Yes, create multiple shipments from the same order and ship partial quantities.
