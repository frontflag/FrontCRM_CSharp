## Overview

The Customer module is used to create, view, edit, and organize all customer records in FrontCRM.

## Common operations

### Create a customer

1. Navigate to **Customer > Customer List**.
2. Click the **Add** button.
3. Fill in the customer name, contact information, and address.
4. Click **Save**.

### Edit a customer

1. Open the customer list.
2. Click the customer name you want to edit.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Click **Save**.

### Search and filter

- Use the search box at the top of the list to find customers by name or code.
- Use the filter panel to narrow results by status, region, or sales owner.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I import customers from Excel?**
A: Yes, use the **Import** button on the customer list page and follow the template format.

**Q: How do I deactivate a customer?**
A: Edit the customer and set the status to **Inactive**. The record will remain available for historical reference.
