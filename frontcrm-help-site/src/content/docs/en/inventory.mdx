## Overview

The Inventory module tracks stock levels, warehouse movements, and inventory adjustments.

## Common operations

### Check stock

1. Navigate to **Inventory > Stock List**.
2. Use the search box to find a product.
3. View the available quantity and reserved quantity.

### Stock in / stock out

1. Open the **Inventory Movement** page.
2. Select the movement type (In / Out / Transfer).
3. Enter the product, quantity, and warehouse.
4. Confirm.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between available quantity and on-hand quantity?**
A: On-hand quantity is the physical stock in the warehouse. Available quantity excludes reserved stock for open orders.
