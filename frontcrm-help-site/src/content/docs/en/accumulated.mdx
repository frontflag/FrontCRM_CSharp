## Overview

The Accumulated module provides aggregated statistics and historical data across modules.

## Common operations

### View accumulated reports

1. Navigate to **Accumulated > Accumulated Report**.
2. Select the date range and grouping criteria.
3. Click **Query** to generate the report.

### Export data

1. Open the accumulated report.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Choose the file format (Excel or PDF).

## FAQ

**Q: What data is included in accumulated reports?**
A: Accumulated reports can include sales, purchase, inventory, and financial summaries depending on the configuration.
