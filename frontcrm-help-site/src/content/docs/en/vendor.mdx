## Overview

The Vendor module stores supplier information and tracks purchase history.

## Common operations

### Create a vendor

1. Navigate to **Vendor > Vendor List**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Enter the vendor name, contact person, and payment terms.
4. Click **Save**.

### Edit a vendor

1. Open the vendor list.
2. Click the vendor name.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Click **Save**.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I link a vendor to a purchase order?**
A: When creating a purchase order, select the vendor from the dropdown or search by vendor code.
