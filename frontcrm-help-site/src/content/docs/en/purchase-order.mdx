## Overview

The Purchase Order module manages orders placed with suppliers, including approval, receipt, and payment tracking.

## Common operations

### Create a purchase order

1. Navigate to **Purchase > Purchase Order List**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Select the vendor and add purchase lines.
4. Save and submit for approval.

### Receive goods

1. Open the purchase order.
2. Click **Receive**.
3. Enter the received quantity and warehouse.
4. Confirm.

## FAQ

**Q: Can a purchase order be created from a sales order?**
A: Yes, use the **Procurement** action on a sales order to generate related purchase orders.
