## Overview

The RFQ & Quote module handles customer inquiries and supplier quotations before a sales order is confirmed.

## Common operations

### Create an RFQ

1. Navigate to **RFQ > RFQ List**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Select the customer and enter product requirements.
4. Save the RFQ.

### Create a quote

1. From an RFQ, click **Create Quote**.
2. Add pricing, quantity, and validity period.
3. Submit or print the quote.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between an RFQ and a quote?**
A: An RFQ is a customer request for pricing. A quote is the formal offer you send back to the customer.
