## Overview

The Dashboard is your home page after logging in. It provides a quick overview of key business metrics and pending tasks.

## Common operations

### View widgets

The dashboard typically includes:

- Pending approvals
- Recent sales orders
- Recent purchase orders
- Inventory alerts
- Key performance indicators

### Navigate to modules

Click any card or menu item on the dashboard to jump to the related module.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I customize the dashboard layout?**
A: Dashboard customization depends on your system configuration and permissions. Contact your administrator if you need changes.
